What happens when you log in

  1. Your browser sends POST /login with the form in the request body.
  2. The server checks your password against a salted PBKDF2-SHA256 hash.
  3. It answers 302 Found with Set-Cookie: sqms_session=<random token>.
  4. From then on, every request carries Cookie: sqms_session=..., which is how a stateless protocol remembers you.
