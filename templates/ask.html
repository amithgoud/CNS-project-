What happens when you press Send

  1. Your browser opens a TCP connection and sends POST /ask, with your question URL-encoded in the body.
  2. The server checks your session cookie, saves the question in SQLite with state PENDING_LLM, and answers 302 Found.
  3. Your browser follows the redirect to the question's page, which polls the server every few seconds for progress.
